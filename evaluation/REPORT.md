## Limitations observed in these runs

- Places are occasionally tagged as people: `Meryton` and `Longbourn` reach the top twenty of *Pride and Prejudice* without the ignore list. The application exposes a **Names to ignore** field for exactly this correction.
- A bare surname that is shared by a family stays a separate node (`Bennet`, `Reed`, `Rivers`), because merging it would be a guess. The reference list credits it once, and the app warns when one name appears with several honorifics.
- The small English model occasionally labels a person as an organisation or a place, which costs a few mentions. Larger spaCy models can be substituted with `load_pipeline('en_core_web_trf')` if accuracy matters more than speed.
- Minor characters mentioned once or twice are dropped by the default `minimum_mentions=2` filter; the reference lists include a few of them.
- Co-occurrence is not a relationship type. A recovered edge only says that two names share a paragraph more often than chance; it says nothing about kinship or enmity.
