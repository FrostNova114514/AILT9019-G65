Elizabeth Bennet arrived at Netherfield with Jane Bennet. Mr. Bingley welcomed Jane warmly, while Mr. Darcy spoke briefly with Elizabeth. Lizzy found Darcy proud and distant.

Later, Charlotte Lucas asked Elizabeth about the evening. Elizabeth told Charlotte that Mr. Darcy had refused to dance. Jane, however, believed that Mr. Bingley had been kind to everyone.

At Longbourn, Mrs. Bennet praised Mr. Bingley and encouraged Jane to visit Netherfield. Elizabeth laughed with Jane about their mother's enthusiasm, but she remained uncertain about Darcy.

When Jane became ill, Elizabeth walked to Netherfield to see her. Bingley was concerned for Jane, and Darcy began to notice Elizabeth's intelligence. Miss Bennet stayed with Jane until she recovered.

Charlotte later warned Elizabeth that first impressions could be misleading. Elizabeth defended her opinion of Darcy, while Jane continued to speak well of Bingley.
